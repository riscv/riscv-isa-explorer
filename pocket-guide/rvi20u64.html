How to read this guide

Each extension page

  1. Edge tab on the right names the catalogue group, so a closed guide can be thumbed by section.
  2. Name, status and version, and the ratification date when there is one.
  3. Does, Use for, and Needs: the extensions it directly requires.
  4. Encodings of up to 10 instructions; the rest are in the online explorer.
  5. Tools: the first Clang, GCC, binutils, QEMU and Linux releases that accept it.

Fixed bits are dark; operand bits are grey dashes. 32-bit encodings are split funct7 | rs2 | rs1 | funct3 | rd | opcode, bit 31 first. Compressed (16-bit) encodings are shown whole.

Numbers

A small number beside an extension name is the page it is on. A * on a tool version means the oldest release checked already had it, so support may be older. The index is at the back.
